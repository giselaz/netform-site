Inside the NX-24

One rack unit. 28 ports.

The NX-24 is a managed edge switch for offices, hotels and server rooms. Every part you see is designed and assembled by our team. Scroll to take it apart.

1U19″ rack28Ports3.9 kgWeight
Enclosure

A cover built to breathe

The 1.2 mm steel lid has vent slots along its rear edge. They line up with the fan path, so warm air leaves the case instead of circulating around the board.

1.2 mmSteel14Vent slots
Cooling

Cool at full load

A nine-fin aluminium heatsink sits directly on the switching chip. Three hot-swap fans at the rear pull air across it. Below 30 °C room temperature the fans stop and the unit runs silent.

3Hot-swap fans<30 °CFanless mode
Mainboard

128 Gb/s on one chip

The switching chip forwards traffic on every port at full speed at the same time. VLANs and routing rules run in hardware, so adding rules doesn't slow the network down.

128 Gb/sCapacity95.2 MppsForwarding
Port module

24 ports, 4 uplinks

Twenty-four 1 GbE ports connect desks, cameras and access points. Four SFP+ slots carry 10 Gb/s fibre uplinks to the core. Each port has its own link light, so faults are visible at a glance.

24 × 1GRJ454 × 10GSFP+
Power supply

Power that stays on

A 150 W, 80 PLUS Gold supply feeds the board and powers PoE devices on the ports. A second supply can be added for full redundancy.
